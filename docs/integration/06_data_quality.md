# Data Quality Audit Metrics

Every integration execution tracks comprehensive telemetry quality metrics:

- `records_generated`: Total CAN frames generated by TelemetryEncoder.
- `records_published`: Total CAN frames submitted to transport layer.
- `records_received`: Total CAN frames received by the CAN Transport layer.
- `records_ingested`: Total packets successfully ingested by IngestionPipeline.
- `records_failed`: Total ingestion failures.
- `records_dropped`: Total packets dropped due to transport backpressure.
- `records_persisted`: Total valid measurements written to Module 01 storage.
