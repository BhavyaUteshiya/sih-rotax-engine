# Module 02 — 17. Physically Closed Turbocharger & Turbine Dynamics

## 1. Physical Shaft Dynamics & Governing Differential Equations

$$J_{\text{turbo}} \frac{d\omega_{\text{turbo}}}{dt} = \tau_{\text{turbine}} - \tau_{\text{compressor}} - \tau_{\text{turbo\_friction}}$$

where:
- $J_{\text{turbo}}$ ($\text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$): Turbocharger shaft rotational inertia (loaded from YAML, e.g. $0.00008\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$)
- $\tau_{\text{turbine}}$ ($\text{N}\cdot\text{m}$): Turbine aerodynamic driving torque
- $\tau_{\text{compressor}}$ ($\text{N}\cdot\text{m}$): Compressor thermodynamic load torque
- $\tau_{\text{turbo\_friction}}$ ($\text{N}\cdot\text{m}$): Turbo shaft mechanical friction torque

---

## 2. Turbine Power Extraction & Driving Torque

Inputs from Phase 3.3.1 Exhaust Subsystem:
- $\dot{m}_{\text{exh}}$ ($\text{kg/s}$): Total exhaust gas mass flow rate
- $T_{\text{exh}}$ ($\text{K}$): Exhaust gas temperature
- $\dot{E}_{\text{exh}}$ ($\text{W}$): Available exhaust thermal energy rate

Turbine Aerodynamic Power Output:
$$P_{\text{turbine}} = \eta_{\text{turbine}} \cdot (1.0 - w_{\text{wastegate}}) \cdot \dot{E}_{\text{exh}} \quad [\text{W}]$$
Enforcing energy conservation: $0 \le P_{\text{turbine}} \le \dot{E}_{\text{exh}}$.

Turbine Driving Torque:
$$\tau_{\text{turbine}} = \frac{P_{\text{turbine}}}{\max(50.0, \omega_{\text{turbo}})} \quad [\text{N}\cdot\text{m}]$$
Low-speed effective angular velocity ($\max(50.0, \omega_{\text{turbo}})$) prevents numerical division singularities as $\omega_{\text{turbo}} \to 0$.

---

## 3. Compressor Thermodynamic Work & Closed-Loop MAP Generation

Compressor Pressure Ratio ($\Pi_c = P_{\text{manifold}} / P_{\text{ambient}}$):
$$\Pi_c = 1.0 + (\Pi_{c,\text{max}} - 1.0) \cdot \left(\min\left(1.0, \frac{\omega_{\text{turbo}}}{\omega_{\text{turbo,max}}}\right)\right)^2$$

Closed-Loop Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP):
$$P_{\text{manifold}} = \min(P_{\text{MAP,max}}, P_{\text{ambient}} \cdot \Pi_c) \quad [\text{Pa}]$$
Gauge Boost Pressure:
$$P_{\text{boost\_gauge}} = \max(0.0, P_{\text{manifold}} - P_{\text{ambient}}) \quad [\text{Pa}]$$

Compressor Isentropic Discharge Temperature Rise:
$$T_{\text{comp\_out}} = T_{\text{ambient}} \cdot \left[1.0 + \frac{1}{\eta_{\text{compressor}}} \left( \Pi_c^{\frac{\gamma - 1}{\gamma}} - 1.0 \right)\right] \quad [\text{K}]$$

Intercooler Thermal Outlet Temperature:
$$T_{\text{manifold}} = T_{\text{ambient}} + (1.0 - \epsilon_{\text{intercooler}}) \cdot (T_{\text{comp\_out}} - T_{\text{ambient}}) \quad [\text{K}]$$
where $\epsilon_{\text{intercooler}} = 0.85$ (configurable intercooler effectiveness loaded from YAML).

Compressor Power Requirement & Load Torque:
$$P_{\text{compressor}} = \frac{\dot{m}_{\text{air}} \cdot C_{p,\text{air}} \cdot (T_{\text{comp\_out}} - T_{\text{ambient}})}{\eta_{\text{compressor}}} \quad [\text{W}]$$
$$\tau_{\text{compressor}} = \frac{P_{\text{compressor}}}{\max(50.0, \omega_{\text{turbo}})} \quad [\text{N}\cdot\text{m}]$$

## 4. Turbo Shaft Mechanical Friction Torque Model

$$\tau_{\text{turbo\_friction}} = \tau_{\text{tf,static}} \cdot \tanh(10 \cdot \omega_{\text{turbo}}) + c_{\text{tf,viscous}} \cdot \omega_{\text{turbo}} + c_{\text{tf,hydro}} \cdot \omega_{\text{turbo}}^2$$
All friction coefficients stream strictly from YAML configuration (`ESTIMATED`, `calibration_required: true`).
