# Phase 1C: References

1. **EASA.E.122 — Rotax 914 Engine series**
   - Source for baseline engine geometry (Bore, Stroke, Displacement, Cylinders).
   - [URL](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/type-certificates/engine-cs-e/easae122-rotax-914-engine-series)
   
2. **Performance prediction of aircraft gasoline turbocharged engine at high-altitudes**
   - Authors: Hossein Mansouri, Fatholah Ommi
   - Journal: Applied Thermal Engineering, 2019
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2019.04.116
   - Relevance: Methodological framework for applying one-dimensional engine modeling (including throttle, inlet manifold, cylinders) to a turbocharged Rotax 914 engine to evaluate volumetric efficiency and mass flow. Used to justify the general shape of the dependencies.
   - [URL](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1359431118380268)

3. **The Analysis of Mean Value SI Engine Models**
   - Authors: Hendricks & Vesterholm
   - Journal: SAE 920682
   - Relevance: Supports the use of speed-density formulations and generic physical flow restrictions (MVEM methodology).
   - [URL](https://doi.org/10.4271/920682)

4. **Modelling of the Intake Manifold Filling Dynamics**
   - Journal: SAE 960037
   - Relevance: Supports methodology used for intake manifold charging and restriction flows.
