# Phase 1A: Atmosphere References

This document records the external authoritative sources used specifically for the Phase 1A Atmosphere model.

## [REF-ATM-01] ICAO Standard Atmosphere (1993)
- **Title:** Manual of the ICAO Standard Atmosphere (extended to 80 kilometres (262 500 feet))
- **Author/Organization:** International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO)
- **Source Type:** International Standard (Doc 7488/3)
- **Information Used:** Base parameters ($T_0, P_0, L$), the linear temperature lapse equation, and the integrated hydrostatic pressure equation for the Troposphere.
- **Authority Level:** HIGHEST (Global Aviation Standard)
- **Date Accessed:** September 2026

## [REF-ATM-02] CODATA Recommended Values of the Fundamental Physical Constants
- **Title:** CODATA Recommended Values of the Fundamental Physical Constants
- **Author/Organization:** Committee on Data for Science and Technology (CODATA)
- **Source Type:** Scientific Standard
- **Information Used:** Universal Gas Constant ($R$).
- **Authority Level:** HIGHEST (Fundamental Physics Standard)
- **Date Accessed:** September 2026

## [REF-ATM-03] Alduchov and Eskridge Magnus Formula
- **Title:** Improved Magnus Form Approximation of Saturation Vapor Pressure
- **Author/Organization:** Alduchov, O.A. and Eskridge, R.E. (Journal of Applied Meteorology)
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0450(1996)035<0601:IMFAOS>2.0.CO;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0450(1996)035<0601:IMFAOS>2.0.CO;2)
- **Source Type:** Peer-reviewed Journal Article (1996)
- **Information Used:** The $6.1078 \times 10^{\frac{7.5 \times T}{T + 237.3}}$ coefficients for saturation vapor pressure of water over liquid.
- **Authority Level:** HIGH (Standard meteorological approximation)
- **Date Accessed:** September 2026
