# Phase 1D: References

- **EASA Rotax 914 Type Certificate (E.122):** Used to confirm basic layout and performance benchmarks.
- **Rotax Operator Documentation:** Used for nominal fuel consumption verification (~33 L/h at takeoff load).
- **Rotax Parts Catalog / System Manual:** Identified fuel pressure operating bounds.
- **Hendricks & Vesterholm (SAE 920682):** Basis for the Mean Value Engine Modeling (MVEM) structure, including fueling surrogates and thermal partitioning assumptions.
- **Mansouri & Ommi (2019):** Altitude thermodynamic effects and validation targets.
