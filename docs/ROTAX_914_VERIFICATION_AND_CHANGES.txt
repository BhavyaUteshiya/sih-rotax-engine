==================================================
PHASE 4: SEVERE VIOLATION / HARD-LIMIT DEBOUNCE BYPASS
==================================================

1. ISSUE IDENTIFIED
During the final verification audit, it was discovered that the 2.0-second debounce timer in ResidualAnalyzer suppressed ALL warnings equally, including severe deviations and critical hard-limit violations (e.g. RPM > 5800).

2. RESOLUTION
- Updated src/digital_twin/analysis/residual_analyzer.py to parse physical operating safety limits directly from configs/module02/engines/rotax_914.yaml
- Implemented a severe violation check in ResidualAnalyzer.analyze().
- If an observed value crosses a documented max operating limit (e.g., max_takeoff_rpm = 5800.0, max_safe_cht_k = 408.15K), the warning is flagged immediately (bypassing the 2.0s transient debounce timer).
- Normal threshold excursions (e.g. RPM deviation of 150 but absolute RPM < 5800) continue to use the 2.0s debounce timer.

3. VALIDATION
Created a unit test to validate Conditions A, B, C, and D:
- Condition A: Short transient (1.5s duration) triggers 0 warnings and properly clears tracking state.
- Condition B: Persistent violation (>2.0s) properly triggers warnings after the debounce timer expires.
- Condition C: Severe hard-limit violation (e.g. RPM=5850) triggers the warning instantaneously, bypassing the debounce timer.
- Condition D: Engine 1 and Engine 2 maintain completely independent debounce and tracking states.

4. END OF INTEGRATION
The Rotax 914 Digital Twin Integration is complete.
